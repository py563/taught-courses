{{< intro >}}
This site lists courses and teaching materials by [Prajwal Yashasvi](https://prajwalyashasvi.in/). We want everyone to be able to use it, including people who rely on assistive technology.
{{< /intro >}}

## Our goal

We aim to meet [WCAG 2.1](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG21/) level AA where practical. The site is built with the [Dot Org Hugo theme](https://github.com/cncf/dot-org-hugo-theme), which includes skip links, semantic markup, and keyboard-friendly navigation.

## What we do on this site

- **Skip to content** :- available at the top of every page for keyboard and screen-reader users.
- **Readable structure** :- headings, lists, and links are used so content makes sense when read in order.
- **Contact form** :- labels are associated with inputs on the [Contact](/contact/) page.
- **Search** :- site search is available from the header when enabled.

## Known limitations

- Some course material may be linked as **PDFs or slides** hosted elsewhere; those files may not meet the same accessibility standards as this site.
- **Colour contrast** or **font size** preferences may not match every user’s needs everywhere on the site.
- We are still adding content; new pages may occasionally ship before a full accessibility pass.

If something blocks you from using the site, please tell us so we can fix it or provide the information another way.

## Feedback

Tell us about accessibility problems:

- Use the [Contact](/contact/) page, or email [work@prajwalyashasvi.in](mailto:work@prajwalyashasvi.in).
- Include the page URL and what you were trying to do (for example, “screen reader could not activate the search button”).

We will try to respond within a reasonable time and improve the site based on your report.

## Assessment

This statement was prepared for the current version of the site. Conformance is based on self-review and theme defaults; we have not engaged a formal third-party audit.
