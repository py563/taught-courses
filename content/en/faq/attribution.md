title: "How should I credit you?"
weight: 2
---

Please **mention my name** when you reuse or share material from this site. A clear attribution helps others find the source and keeps academic reuse honest.

**Suggested credit line:**

> Material by **Prajwal Yashasvi Maredukonda**, from [teaching.prajwalyashasvi.in](https://teaching.prajwalyashasvi.in/) (or the specific page URL you used).

For slides or handouts, a short footer or references slide is enough. For repos or course sites, link to the original page when possible.

If you adapt content heavily, say that it is **based on** or **adapted from** my materials so readers know the origin.
