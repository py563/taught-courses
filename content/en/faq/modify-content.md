---
title: "May I edit or adapt the content?"
weight: 4
---

For **non-commercial educational use**, you may adapt slides, examples, or notes for your class, trim sections, translate explanations, or update code for a newer stack, provided you [credit me](/faq/attribution/) and link to this site when practical.

If you **publish** adapted work beyond your own classroom (e.g. public GitHub, another college’s open courseware), keep attribution and note that your version is adapted.

For **commercial** adaptations, treat it as [commercial use](/faq/commercial-use/) and get permission first.
