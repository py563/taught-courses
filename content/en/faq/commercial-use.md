**Not without permission.** Commercial use includes (but is not limited to) paid courses, corporate training sold to clients, books or publications for sale, SaaS products, or any setting where **you or your organisation profit** from the material in a way that goes beyond ordinary non-commercial teaching.

**Please request permission beforehand** by contacting me via the [Contact](/contact/) page or [work@prajwalyashasvi.in](mailto:work@prajwalyashasvi.in). Include:

- What material you want to use (links or titles)
- How you plan to use it (product, audience, distribution)
- Whether you will modify it
- Your organisation and timeline

I will respond as soon as I can. Until you have written approval, commercial use is **not** licensed.
