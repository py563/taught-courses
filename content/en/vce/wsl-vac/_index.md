## Pre-Requirements for Course

- Laptop with Windows 10 (``` Ver 2004+ aka Build 19041+ ```) or Windows 11.
- Minimal understanding of Unix/Linux OS and Shell Commands.
- Github account (recommended with your Vardhaman email address).
- Optional: Update Windows 10 to ``` Build 20262+```.
