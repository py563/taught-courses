## Textbook

- J. L. Bayuk, J. Healey, P. Rohmeyer, M. Sachs, J. Schmidt, and J. Weiss, *Cyber Security Policy Guidebook*. John Wiley & Sons, 2012..

## Reference books

- R. A. Clarke and R. Knake, *Cyberwar: The Next Threat to National Security & What to Do About It*. Ecco, 2010.
- D. Shoemaker, *Cyber Security: The Essential Body of Knowledge*, 1st ed. Boston, MA, USA: Cengage Learning, 2011.
- M. Shema, *Anti-Hacker Tool Kit*, Indian ed. McGraw Hill.
- N. Godbole and S. Belpure, *Cyber Security: Understanding Cyber Crimes, Computer Forensics and Legal Perspectives*. Wiley.
- R. Howard, *Cyber Security Essentials*. Auerbach Publications, 2011.
